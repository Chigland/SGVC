02 / The idea

Motivation

From latent Video DiT
to frame-by-frame Pixel DiT.

Image diffusion models have limited native temporal modeling. Clip-wise video DiTs capture richer spatiotemporal dependencies, but require multi-frame buffering and a VAE decoder to reconstruct pixels.

SGVC adapts a pretrained latent video DiT into a causal pixel-space refiner. It aligns codec features with the DiT's input embeddings, and reconstructs each frame directly through a lightweight pixel reconstruction path.
